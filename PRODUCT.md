# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Parents of CBSE Class 1-10 students in India seeking personalized, diagnostic AI learning, high academic performance, and clear visibility into their child's daily progress without endless tuition classes or rigid lecture schedules.

## Product Purpose

InstaSkills GameChanger (IGr) is an AI-powered personalized learning platform for CBSE students that diagnoses learning gaps in real time, creates tailored learning pathways, and delivers observable grade improvements within 40 hours.

## Positioning

Unlike conventional edtech apps that force students into linear video lectures or generic question banks, IGr adapts dynamically to each learner's cognitive pace, curiosity level, and visual/conceptual strengths, providing parents with a real-time analytics dashboard.

## Operating Context

Mobile-first and desktop web interface accessed by parents on smartphones and tablets, and by students on laptops/tablets for daily learning sessions, diagnostic assessments, and progress tracking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- AI diagnostic assessment & personal learning studio (APPU Learning Studio)
- Real-time Parent Dashboard (concept mastery, speed, accuracy, weak spots)
- 40-hour visible results methodology
- CBSE curriculum alignment (Math, Science, English, STEM)
- Responsive mobile UX (touch-optimized navigation, thumb-friendly CTAs, quick bottom-sheet modals, zero horizontal overflow)

## Brand Commitments

- Name: IGr (InstaSkills GameChanger)
- Palette: Warm architectural palette (#FAF8F5 deep off-white background, #134340 deep emerald green primary, #111C1A charcoal headers, subtle warm accents)
- Tone: High-trust, editorial, empathetic yet authoritative

## Evidence on Hand

- Real copy, structured comparison charts, parent dashboard previews, student success statistics (40h visible progress guarantee), and interactive video showcase.

## Product Principles

1. Mobile-First Ergonomics: Every card, table, modal, and button must feel natively crafted for 375px–430px viewports (touch targets >=44px, sticky mobile CTA bar, stackable layouts).
2. Editorial Luxury Aesthetic: Clean typography, crisp double-borders (Doppelrand), high-contrast legibility, and zero visual clutter.
3. Micro-Interaction Polish: Tactile feedback, smooth gesture reveals, pull-up modals, and responsive data visualizers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliant color contrast
- Responsive text scale without truncation or clipping on mobile viewports
- Screen reader friendly ARIA labels on touch controls
